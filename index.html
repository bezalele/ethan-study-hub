A bold statement. A new nation. A big question: now what?

On July 4, 1776, the thirteen colonies declared that they were no longer part of Britain. The Declaration of Independence explained why — and laid out powerful ideas about liberty, rights, and government.

“We hold these truths to be self-evident,
that all men are created equal…”— Declaration of Independence (1776)

FOUNDING STORY

How did a country get built from disagreement?

One tension drives the founding: How do you make government strong enough to work, but limited enough to protect liberty?

🧠 Ethan's mental model

Britain felt too powerful → Articles were too weak → Constitution seeks balance.
