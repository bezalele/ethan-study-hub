UNIT 1 · THE STORY

How did America get here?

One tension drives the founding: How do you make government strong enough to work, but limited enough to protect liberty?

1607–1775
⚓

British Colonies

Thirteen colonies develop local institutions, but remain under British authority.

The problem grows: Who should have the right to govern the colonists?
1776
📜

Declaration of Independence

The colonies explain why they are separating from Britain.

Core idea: natural rights + consent of the governed.
1781–1789
🤝

Articles of Confederation

Fear of concentrated power produces a deliberately weak national government.

Problem: Congress cannot tax directly; national coordination is weak.
1787
🏛️

Constitutional Convention

Delegates design a stronger national system while dividing and checking power.

Big debate: federal power, representation, liberty.
1788–1791
⚖️

Constitution + Bill of Rights

Ratification establishes the new framework; the first ten amendments protect key liberties.

The experiment: power, divided and constrained.

🧠 Ethan's mental model

Britain felt too powerful → Articles were too weak → Constitution seeks balance.
